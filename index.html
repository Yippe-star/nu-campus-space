Quick demo

  1. Sign in with the demo account.
  2. Tap Book 14:00–16:00 next to a free room.
  3. Tap I'm at the desk: check me in, or scan the code in the Circulation Desk tab.
  4. Open the Lobby screen: the room turns Occupied.
  5. Tap Finish now. The room turns Free.
